# Types for planets with life

Currently the types (label) and descriptions of planets does not change for planets with life, this creates inconsistencies like Jungle planets without life or with microbiobial life.

## Solution
Planet with life on them should have a label and description based both on the planet type and the life complexity.
For example a jungle planet without life could be "Planet with permanent cloud cover and endless rain, devoid of life" while one with microbibial lefe could be "Planet with permanent cloud cover and endless rain, the surface of the planet is covered in thick layer of bacteria, fungi or algae".
Take these as example and elaborate better labels and descriptions.

All planets potentially with lifeshould be impacted by this changes. The internal tyoe of the planets must not change, only label and description. Seed invariance must be respected.
